# Code of Conduct

NEO follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: treat people with respect; assume good intent; criticize ideas, not authors; no harassment, doxxing, or personal attacks.

Reports: open a private issue or email stevenjunop@gmail.com. Maintainers will respond within 7 days.
